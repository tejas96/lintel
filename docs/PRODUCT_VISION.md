# Product Vision — Lintel

> A lintel is the beam above a doorway: quiet, structural, and the reason the
> opening holds. Lintel is that beam for an interior design and architecture studio.

## The problem

Studios run on the owner's memory. Priorities live in chat threads, deadlines in
spreadsheets, approvals in someone's inbox, and leave in a calendar nobody checks
before promising a client a date. The result is predictable: missed milestones,
hidden blockers, conflicting priorities, constant follow-ups, and an owner who
cannot step away.

## The product

Lintel is a calm, configurable **business execution system** — not a generic task
manager. It coordinates projects, workflows, tasks, approvals, capacity, leave,
notifications and recognition so that the studio runs on clear workflows instead
of constant intervention.

**Central principle: make the right action the easiest action.**

| Person | Lintel answers, at a glance |
|---|---|
| Employee / designer | What is my next priority, and why? What is due, overdue, blocked, waiting on review? |
| Site supervisor | What must I inspect today? Where do I log photos, issues and handovers? |
| Team lead / reviewer | What is waiting for my review? Who is blocked? |
| Project manager | Is my project on track? Which dependencies, approvals and people put the milestone at risk? |
| Owner | How healthy is the business? What decisions need me — and only me? |

## Outcomes we design for (measured, not promised)

1. More projects delivered on time.
2. Explicit priorities and single accountable owners.
3. Blockers and delivery risk surfaced early.
4. A clear daily plan for every person.
5. Workflows that adapt to project types.
6. Less routine owner intervention.
7. Visible leave and capacity before commitments are made.
8. Quality protected by review and approval steps.
9. Rewards for meaningful outcomes, not raw task volume.
10. Transparent information for the right people, private by default.
11. Approachable for people unfamiliar with complex software.
12. A reliable foundation for AI assistance in Phase 2.

The command center reports on-time delivery rate with its sample size and the
previous period, so the studio can see whether these outcomes actually improve.

## Experience principles

- **Calm, premium, alive.** Warm paper surfaces, one confident accent, purposeful
  motion that explains cause and effect ("Linen & Cyanotype", see `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`).
- **Explain, never surprise.** Every recommendation carries its reason; priorities
  never reorder silently; disabled actions say why.
- **Progressive disclosure.** The first screen shows what needs you; detail is one tap away.
- **Honest data.** Every number comes from records with a documented formula. Empty
  states say what to configure. Nothing is faked.
- **Respect people.** Leave is never rejected by software; work is never silently
  reassigned; performance is judged by configurable, explained rules.

## Phases

- **Phase 1 (this build):** the complete operational product — identity, projects,
  workflow templates, tasks, daily planner, deliverables and approvals, leave,
  capacity, notifications with durable reminders and escalations, rewards, owner
  command center, web and mobile apps.
- **Phase 2:** AI assistance on top of the same permissions and use cases —
  natural-language status questions, delay explanations, risk summaries, workload
  suggestions, draft plans. Disabled in Phase 1 by design (`AI_PHASE_TWO.md`).
